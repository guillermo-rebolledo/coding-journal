# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, offer constructive feedback, and prioritize the safety and dignity of other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person’s private information without permission. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
